Privacy Policy

Last updated: to be confirmed at publication · Draft pending review by counsel

This is a draft. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be published live until it has been checked against how the Foundation actually collects and stores data (its email provider, form backend, donation processor, and any analytics tool it ends up using).

  1. Who we are

  4. Cookies

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy covers the website of External Excellencies Global Foundation ("the Foundation," "we," "us"), including pages describing our history, our work and impact, and how to get involved. It does not cover third-party sites we link to, such as news coverage, partner organizations, or KEMRI and government publications referenced in our Impact section.

2. What information we collect

We only collect information that a visitor gives us directly. Depending on which forms are active on the site, that can include:

  • Name and email address, if you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Name, email address, and any message content, if you use the contact form
  • Name, contact details, and any details you provide, if you apply to volunteer or partner with us
  • Payment and donor information, if and when a donation processor is connected — this policy will be updated with the processor's own data-handling terms before that feature goes live

We do not currently collect information automatically through analytics or tracking scripts. If that changes, this section will be updated to name the tool and what it collects.

3. How we use it

We use the information you provide to respond to your message, send the newsletter you signed up for, process a volunteer or partnership inquiry, or process a donation. We do not use your information for anything you haven't asked for, and we do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies

This site does not currently set marketing or advertising cookies. Any cookies used for basic site functionality (such as remembering that you closed a banner) will be listed here by name once they are in use.

5. Sharing information

We share information only where necessary to provide a service you requested — for example, with an email delivery provider to send our newsletter, or with a payment processor to handle a donation once one is connected. We do not share your information with partner organizations, KEMRI, government bodies, or media outlets referenced elsewhere on this site.

6. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. Because the specific hosting, email, and form-processing tools for this site are still being finalized, the technical detail in this section (where data is stored, for how long, and using which safeguards) needs to be completed before this policy is published live.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it — for example, by unsubscribing from the newsletter or emailing us directly. If you are in a jurisdiction with a specific data-protection law (such as Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019), you may have further rights under that law; this section will be expanded once counsel confirms which obligations apply to the Foundation.

8. Children's information

Some of our programs serve youth and students, but this website itself is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website's forms. Any photography or storytelling involving minors from our programs is governed separately by our Safeguarding Policy, including consent requirements before any image or story involving a minor is published.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through the Contact page.
