Why we exist

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

Across the communities we work in, we meet people with the ambition, discipline and ideas to change their circumstances — but without consistent access to capital, training, healthcare or a network that opens doors. External Excellencies Global Foundation exists to close that gap: not through one-time handouts, but through pathways that women, youth and communities can build a future on.

From vision to impact

Our story so far

  1. 2017

    External Excellencies Women's Ministry begins — empowering women and youth through entrepreneurship seminars, medical camps and community outreach.

  2. 2023

    The work expands into External Excellencies Global Foundation — a broader, more structured platform for empowerment across health, education, skills and community development.

  3. 2025

    Expanded community health initiatives, including the South B Community Medical Camp in partnership with KEMRI, the Betting Control and Licensing Board, and MobiLab Africa.

  4. 2026 →

    Building the next chapter.
