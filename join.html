◆ You Belong Here

A Place
Prepared for You

Whether you are just beginning your faith journey or you've walked with God for decades — there is a place for you in EEWM. We are a community that honours every woman's story, meets her where she is, and walks with her toward all that God has prepared.

From our monthly Second Saturday gatherings to our online community, prayer groups, and mentorship circles, you will find connection, growth, and genuine sisterhood here.
