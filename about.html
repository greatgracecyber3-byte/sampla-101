◆ Who We Are

EEWM at a Glance

Eternal Excellencies Women Ministry (EEWM) is a faith-based women's discipleship and empowerment movement rooted in Nairobi, Kenya. Founded on the conviction that every woman carries a God-given excellence within her, we walk alongside women to awaken, develop, and release that potential — for the glory of God and the good of generations to come.

◆ Our Foundations ◆
✨

Our Vision

A Woman Fully Alive in God

We envision a generation of women who know who they are in Christ — women of unshakeable identity, bold faith, and transformative influence in their homes, churches, communities, and nations. We see a movement that outlasts us, a joy of many generations.

"She is clothed with strength and dignity; she can laugh at the days to come." — Proverbs 31:25

🌟

Our Mission

Disciple. Empower. Connect.

EEWM exists to disciple women in the Word of God, empower them with practical tools for life and purpose, and connect them in authentic community — so that together, they flourish in every dimension of their calling as daughters of the Most High.

"Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them… teaching them to observe all that I have commanded." — Matthew 28:19–20

◆ Built on Solid Ground

Our Three Pillars

Every programme, gathering, and resource at EEWM flows from three pillars that define how we walk with women toward wholeness and purpose.

pillar‑discipleship.jpg

01

◆ First Pillar

Discipleship

We believe every woman is called to grow deeper in the Word of God — not just to know scripture, but to be transformed by it. Through structured Bible studies, mentorship circles, and one-on-one accountability, EEWM creates environments where women encounter God authentically and are shaped into His image.

Discipleship at EEWM is never a solitary journey. We walk together — the seasoned guiding the new, the young energising the established — in a chain of grace that spans generations.

"Go and make disciples of all nations… teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you." — Matthew 28:19–20

pillar‑empowerment.jpg

02

◆ Second Pillar

Empowerment

Faith without action is incomplete. EEWM equips women with practical tools — entrepreneurial skills, financial literacy, leadership development, and health education — ensuring that spiritual growth translates into tangible transformation in her home, workplace, and community.

We champion the Proverbs 31 woman: not as an impossible standard, but as a beautiful picture of a woman whose faith energises her excellence in every arena of life.

"She sees that her trading is profitable, and her lamp does not go out at night." — Proverbs 31:18

pillar‑community.jpg

03

◆ Third Pillar

Community

Iron sharpens iron. EEWM is a sisterhood — a safe, joyful, and honest community where women are known, celebrated, and carried. We create spaces where vulnerability is welcomed, victories are shared, and no woman walks alone through the challenges of life.

From our flagship Second Saturday gatherings to intimate small groups, we believe that life-giving community is not optional — it is the soil in which everything else grows.

"As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another." — Proverbs 27:17
