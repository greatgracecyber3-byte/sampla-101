About Us

A ministry that grew into a foundation — without losing what it started for.

Our story

From a women's ministry to a global foundation

Our roots trace back to 2017, to the External Excellencies Women's Ministry — practical, community-level work focused on empowering women and youth. That work included entrepreneurship seminars, women empowerment, youth development, youth media training, medical camps, community outreach, leadership development and economic empowerment, including seed-capital support that helped widows and women start businesses.

By 2023, that work had outgrown its original structure. It became the External Excellencies Global Foundation — a broader platform for the same mission, built to carry women's empowerment, youth development, healthcare, education, skills development, entrepreneurship, leadership and community development further than a ministry alone could reach.

In 2025, that expansion took visible shape in community health work, including the South B Community Medical Camp — a single day, delivered with partners, that reached 921 participants. It's one data point in a longer story we're still writing.

Why we exist

The gap between ability and opportunity

In the communities we work in, we see this pattern again and again: real ability, discipline and ambition, met with limited access to the things that turn ability into a livelihood.

  • Limited access to opportunitySkill without a route to income stays untapped.
  • Economic vulnerabilityWidows, single mothers and youth are often one setback away from crisis.
  • Skills gapsFormal training is often out of reach financially or geographically.
  • Youth unemployment and disconnectionAmbition with nowhere structured to go.
  • Healthcare accessBasic screening and care aren't always within reach.
  • The need for sustainable community developmentInterventions that outlast a single program cycle.

Our conviction

Talent is never the shortage. Access is.

Everything we build — from a single mentoring session to a community health outreach — starts from that belief.

Our vision

To empower women and youth globally for sustainable, impactful lives.

Our mission

To build pathways for women and youth to thrive by offering educational resources, skill development, health support, and social networks that foster economic stability and personal growth.

Our values

What guides how we work

  • Creating pathways for individuals and communities to realize their potential.

  • Operating with honesty, accountability and transparency.

  • Ensuring that people from diverse backgrounds have opportunities to participate and benefit.

  • Encouraging creative and practical approaches to solving community challenges.

  • Working with partners and communities to achieve greater impact.

  • Helping individuals and communities develop the capacity to overcome challenges.

  • Creating solutions that generate lasting social and economic value.

Founder

Rev. Dr. Jane Mwikali Makau

Founder & Visionary Leader — Chairperson, Board of the National Water Harvesting and Storage Authority (NWHSA)

Rev. Dr. Jane Mwikali Makau is a governance leader, entrepreneur, ordained minister, author and women's-empowerment advocate. Her community work laid the foundation for what began as the External Excellencies Women's Ministry in 2017 and grew into External Excellencies Global Foundation in 2023. She currently chairs the Board of the National Water Harvesting and Storage Authority (NWHSA), a national government appointment effective 24 October 2025 for a three-year term.

Biography

Dr. Makau holds an MBA and a BA (Hons) in Business and Management from the University of Sunderland, UK, alongside a Higher National Diploma from Edexcel (UK), a Diploma in Marketing, Sales and Advertising from Graffins College, and an entrepreneurship certificate from Babson College, USA. She is a NITA-certified lead trainer and the Founder and President of Great Grace Empowerment Institute.

Leadership journey

She first served the NWHSA as an Independent Board Director from February 2019 to February 2022, chairing its Strategy, Technical and Business Development Committee and sitting on its Governance, Risk and Audit Committee. She went on to chair the Betting Control and Licensing Board (BCLB) from December 2022, before returning to NWHSA in October 2025 as Board Chairperson — the position she holds today.

Publications

She is the author of 15 inspirational books, including The Strength of a Woman, The Refinement of Personality, The Power of the Blessing, He Made It Another Vessel, Healing Scars No One Can See, Gold in the Fire, Enforcing the Will of God, The Efficacy of the Word, The Kingdom Protocol, Kingdom Excellence, Unbreakable, The Benefits of Pain, The Blessing of the Rejected, and God — A Loving Father, addressing themes of resilience, faith, leadership and personal transformation.

Speaking & recognition

Her honors include Top Women Entrepreneur in Kenya; the 2024 Most Outstanding Licensing Board in Africa (Africa Public Service Optimum Awards); 2024 Best CSR Regulator in Africa (Gaming Tech Summit); 2024 Top 50 Most Influential Women in Gaming in Africa; and the 2025 Regulatory Alliance Award, presented at the AGE Lagos Summit in Nigeria on 6 March 2025. She speaks widely on governance, entrepreneurship and women's empowerment.
