Our Story

From a Small Team to Kenya's Trusted Electrical Experts

Soltech Electrical was founded in 2016 by a team of certified Kenyan electricians who believed that professional, honest, and affordable electrical services should be accessible to everyone — not just large corporations.

What started as a small wiring and repairs outfit in Nairobi has grown into a full-service electrical and solar company, trusted by homeowners, businesses, schools, and organisations across 15+ counties in Kenya.

We've built our reputation on one thing: doing the job properly, on time, and at a price that is fair. Our customers don't just come back — they refer their neighbours, friends, and colleagues to us.
