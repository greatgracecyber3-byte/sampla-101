Programme overview

Why this programme exists

The External Excellencies Global Foundation, in partnership with the Kenya Industrial Research and Development Institute (KIRDI), is implementing the Youth & Women Enterprise Accelerator & Incubator Programme 2026.

The five-day programme is designed to support women and young entrepreneurs as they build, strengthen, grow and scale their enterprises. It provides a structured enterprise-development journey focused on practical business development, incubation, acceleration, value addition, market readiness, mentorship and relevant business linkages.

The programme reflects the Foundation's commitment to creating opportunities that enable women and youth to develop sustainable enterprises and unlock their potential.

Programme focus

Eight areas of the enterprise-development journey

01

Enterprise development

Supporting participants to strengthen their businesses and entrepreneurial capabilities.

02

Business incubation

Providing a structured environment for developing and strengthening emerging enterprises.

03

Business acceleration

Helping entrepreneurs identify opportunities to grow and scale their businesses.

04

Value addition

Encouraging innovation and improved products, services and enterprise opportunities.

05

Market readiness

Preparing enterprises to understand and respond to market opportunities.

06

Mentorship

Providing guidance and learning opportunities throughout the enterprise-development journey.

07

Business & market linkages

Creating relevant connections and opportunities that can support enterprise growth.

08

Showcase & opportunity

Providing opportunities for enterprises to demonstrate their potential and connect with relevant opportunities.
