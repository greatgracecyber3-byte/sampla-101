Impact & Community Projects

Transforming lives. Empowering communities. Building sustainable futures.

This page brings together the women's empowerment, education, health, water and community-development work led by Dr. Jane Mwikali Makau, the External / Eternal Excellencies Women's Ministry (since 2017), and External Excellencies Global Foundation (established 2023). Each project below links out to news coverage or a partner's report where one exists, alongside our own records.

Organizational history

Two chapters of one story, kept distinct

The women's empowerment and community-outreach work documented here dates back to 2017, under the External / Eternal Excellencies Women's Ministry. External Excellencies Global Foundation was established in 2023 as an expansion of that earlier work — it was not founded in 2017, and this page does not claim otherwise. The earlier ministry ran five entrepreneurship seminars, youth media-training workshops, four medical camps, and seed-capital support for widows and women starting businesses; since 2023 we have added two more medical camps and one empowerment summit.

A Sh10 million borehole was drilled in Mituvu, Makueni County, funded by Betika in partnership with the BCLB. The area Chief publicly thanked Betika and BCLB Chairperson Dr. Jane Makau for the initiative.

Impact: The borehole supplies two schools, a health centre and the chief's camp, addressing chronic water shortages in an arid/semi-arid part of Makueni County.

Project timeline

2017 → 2026

2017

Women's empowerment and community outreach begins under the External / Eternal Excellencies Women's Ministry.

2017–2022

Five entrepreneurship seminars, youth media-training workshops, four medical camps, and seed-capital support for widows and women documented in the ministry's history.

2023

External Excellencies Global Foundation formally established, expanding the ministry's earlier work.

2024

Business/seed-capital initiative for women's groups (International Women's Day); Mituvu, Makueni borehole (Betika/BCLB); Kako Comprehensive School widows & orphans support and borehole (BangBet/BCLB), 18 December.

2025

South B Medical Camp (31 May, KEMRI-verified); Seven-county Free Wellness Medical Camp (March–June); South C Cancer Screening (27 July, KEMRI-verified); water tank donation and borehole drilling initiatives; CSR activity referenced across 32 counties.

2026

Continuing community and leadership impact — this archive will be updated as further verified projects are documented.
