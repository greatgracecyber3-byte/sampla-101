About Our CCTV Service

Full CCTV System Design & Installation

Security starts with seeing what's happening on your property — at any time, from anywhere. Soltech Electrical designs, supplies, and installs complete CCTV systems for homes, businesses, schools, warehouses, and estates across Kenya.

We don't just mount cameras. We assess your site, plan the optimal camera placement for maximum coverage, run all cabling neatly, configure your NVR or DVR, set up your mobile viewing app, and train you on using the system before we leave.

Full system design — camera placement mapped to your property
All cabling installed neatly and concealed where possible
Remote viewing configured on your phone on the day
12-month workmanship warranty on all installations
